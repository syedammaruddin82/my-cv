About

I've spent over fifteen years on the line between complex infrastructure and the people who just need their machine to work. That's meant remote helpdesk work for multi-tenant MSP clients, and thirteen years as the sole on-site technical contact for a manufacturing plant — so I've supported everything from a single confused user to full-site network and directory administration.

Day to day, that looks like triaging Tier 1/2 tickets under SLA pressure, keeping endpoints patched and encrypted, and writing the internal documentation that stops the same question from becoming five more tickets. I'm comfortable owning a problem end-to-end — diagnosis, fix, and the write-up that helps the next person.
